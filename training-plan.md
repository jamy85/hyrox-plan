# Hyrox Plan - 6 Weeks to Race (Sat 29 Aug 2026)

Mon = Row + Strength | Wed = Brick | Fri = Engine + Pull | Sun = 800s (run)
Kit subs: ski -> cable pulldown/bent row | sled -> incline push/ramp | wall ball -> DB thruster.
Rower: every Mon + even-week Fri (W2, W4). Wed brick + odd-week Fri = seated bike or belt.

|---|---|---|

| 800 m goal split | **3:56** (4:56/km) | track-timed, 26 Jul |

| Efficiency factor | ~0.019 | recovering from a 10% dip |

**HR zones off max 170:** recovery <120 | easy 120-136 | steady 137-146 |
threshold 147-158 | VO2/race 159-170.

**Distrust the watch in two places.** On a 400 m track GPS reads ~10% FAST
(showed 4:25-4:36/km when you really ran 4:56) - use a stopwatch on the lap.
On a treadmill the footpod distance is guesswork - trust the belt speed you set.
Heart rate is reliable everywhere; that is why this plan steers by it.

**LOAD RULE (replaces "RIR 3").** Mon 20 Jul at 20 kg goblet / 2x10 kg lunge /
30 kg RDL gave 7-8/10 DOMS and cost the Wed brick. So kilos are decided by last
week's soreness, not by the calendar:
- 0-4/10 and gone by Wednesday -> add ~2 kg lower body
- 5-6/10, or still stiff Wednesday -> repeat the same load
- 7-8/10, or it cost you a session -> drop ~2 kg
- missed last Wednesday -> never add load this week
Within any load, still stop ~3 reps short of failure.

**THE PROGRESS MARKER.** Goal pace stays **fixed at 3:56** all block - you are
never asked to run faster. What should fall is what it costs: last-rep HR 166
(26 Jul) -> 166 (W2) -> 163 (W3) -> ~158 by W5. Same split, a full zone cheaper
= race pace has become sustainable.

## Guardrails
- Compromised running is the whole game - minimise pace drop after leg stations.
- Two hard days only: Wed brick + Sun 800s. Never back-to-back.
- Friday is judged on speed held and evenness, not heart rate (60 s reps peak
  ~140 because HR lags; that is not a soft session).
- If DOMS stacks again, or the same 3:56 starts costing MORE heart rate instead
  of less, turn one hard session easy that week.
- Log every 800s session in the tracker: splits by stopwatch, HR per rep.
