# Hyrox Plan - 6 Weeks to Race (Sat 29 Aug 2026)

Mon = Row + Strength | Wed = Brick | Fri = Engine + Pull | Sun = 800s (run)
800m goal pace ~4:45-5:15/km. Autoregulate loads to RIR 2-3; +2 kg when easy.
Kit subs: ski -> cable pulldown/bent row | sled -> incline push/ramp | wall ball -> DB thruster.
Rower: every Mon + even-week Fri (W2, W4). Wed brick + odd-week Fri = seated bike.

---

## Week 1 - 20-26 Jul (Build)
Reintroduce strength (moderate - expect DOMS). Find your 800m pace.

### Mon - Row + Strength (~65 min)
- [ ] Row intervals: 6x250 m, hard 24-28 spm, rest 90 s
- [ ] Goblet squat: 4x12 @ 20 kg
- [ ] Walking lunge: 4x20 steps @ 2x10 kg
- [ ] Romanian deadlift: 3x12 @ 30 kg
- [ ] DB thruster: 4x15 @ 2x8 kg
- [ ] Cable pulldown / bent row: 4x15 @ RIR 3
- [ ] Core: 3x (40 s plank + 12 mountain climbers)

### Wed - Brick (grooving, no runs yet)
- [ ] 4 rounds: bike -> thruster -> lunge (30-60 s between, 2-3 min between rounds)
- [ ] Seated bike 250-500 m hard (or 12 burpees)
- [ ] DB thruster: 15 @ 2x8-10 kg
- [ ] Walking lunge: 20 steps, BW -> 2x6 kg

### Fri - Engine + Pull (~40 min, no rower this week -> bike)
- [ ] Bike / hard-run intervals: 5x250 m, hard, rest 90 s-2 min
- [ ] Straight-arm pulldown: 3x15 @ RIR 2-3
- [ ] Band pull-apart: 3x20 light
- [ ] Core: Pallof press 3x12/side + hollow hold 3x30 s

### Sun - 800s (outdoor)
- [ ] 5x800 m @ ~4:45-5:15/km, jog 400 m recovery
      "Hard but I could do 5." FIND & LOG your real pace.

### Sat 29 - RACE DAY

## Guardrails
- Compromised running is the whole game - minimise pace drop after leg stations.
- Never two hard days back-to-back. Watch HR/EF for under-recovery.
- Moderate loads W1-2 (DOMS expected). Taper for real in W6.
- Log every 800s session in the tracker so we can watch the pace sharpen.
